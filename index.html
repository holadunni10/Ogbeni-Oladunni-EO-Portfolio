About
Meet Mr. Oladunni OE

With years of experience helping students navigate their academic journey and schools improve their branding and digital presence, Ogbeni Oladunni Oluwasegun Emmanuel stands as one of Lagos’s most trusted educational consultants and creative professionals.

From securing WAEC/NECO results to processing international admissions, and from designing compelling school brands to building modern websites — he provides end-to-end support that students and institutions rely on nationwide.

Over 1,800 students have benefited from his JAMB facilitation services between 2022 and 2026 alone, making him one of the most recognised names in educational consultancy in Lagos State.
